 ‘Son of Captain Redmond Kelly (d. 1798), an Irish soldier who lived in Dean’s Yard, Westminster in later life and sent his three sons to Westminster School. Marshall, Thomas (c. 1789-1874) 
Landed proprietor and entomologist. He spent much of the year 1827 in Keswick, studying the insect population in the surrounding area, and made the acquaintance of the Southey family. He later lived in Birmingham and then Leicester. When Leicester Museum opened in 1849, Marshall donated his collection of over one thousand insects to the Museum. He married Elizabeth Jane Ansell (1789-1855) in 1814 and their son, Thomas Ansell Marshall (1827-1903), became a schoolmaster and clergyman and a well-known entomologist.
