		<cell rendition="#cell1"></cell>
		<cell rendition="#cell2"></cell>

<!-- Model new items after this: -->
		<person xml:id="AbellaManuel">
            <persName>
               <roleName>[title if there is one]</roleName>
               <forename>Manuel</forename>
               			There are most often multiple forenames inside the one tag; legal forenames that the person didn't use are in parentheses before or after the used name in the one forename tag.
               <surname>Abella</surname>
            </persName>
            <birth when="1753"/>
            <death when="1825"/>
               			Can have birth, death, floruit, or none; can contain any symbols (b. d. ? etc.) but NO PARENTHESIS since these
               			are added by the xslt. If no dates, xslt will leave blank; if "(dates unknown)" put it at the end of the bio note.)
            <bibl><title ref="#dnb">DNB</title>, <title ref="#hp">Hist P</title></bibl>
            <note type="bio" target="#AbellaManuel">
               Aragonese lawyer and antiquarian. He served as secretary to the Cortes (1809) and then worked as
               secretary at the Spanish embassy in London. Whilst there he met Blanco White and Southey. On his return to Spain, he supplied
               Southey with material for the <title>Edinburgh Annual Register</title> and his <title>History of the Peninsular War</title>
               (1823–1832). Southey feared that he might have been imprisoned after the restoration of the Spanish monarchy in 1813–14, but
               Abella remained in favour until about 1819, when he was exiled. Until this date he was Southey’s main source of news and
               documents from Spain.
            </note>
        </person>
