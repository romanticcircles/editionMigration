Here is a step-by-step tutorial to transform your raw imported CSV text data into a clear, visually striking network visualization inside Gephi.
## Step 1: Adjust Edge Thickness Scaling
Before running your layout, make the weight values visually obvious on your screen.

   1. Look at the bottom toolbar directly underneath your main graph workspace canvas.
   2. Locate the Edge Thickness Slider (a slider next to a small diagonal line icon).
   3. Drag it slightly to the right. You will instantly notice the lines between BedfordGrosvenorCharles → VincentWilliam (Weight 4) become significantly thicker than the baseline single-interaction lines.
   4. Click the T icon in that same bottom toolbar to turn on node labels so you can see who is who.

## Step 2: Run the ForceAtlas2 Layout
Since your dataset is small, ForceAtlas2 is the best layout algorithm to isolate tightly connected clusters and push unrelated entities away.

   1. Go to the Layout panel on the left side of your screen.
   2. Click the dropdown menu and select ForceAtlas2.
   3. Change these specific settings in the properties list below the dropdown:
   * Check the box for Scaling and increase its value to 15.0 or 20.0 (this spreads the nodes out further so labels do not overlap).
      * Check the box for Prevent Overlap (this keeps nodes from stacking directly on top of each other).
      * Check the box for Edge Weight Influence and set it to 1.5 (this pulls heavily weighted nodes closer together).
   4. Click the Run button. Watch the graph self-organize for a few seconds, then click Stop.

## Step 3: Color Nodes by Connection Density (Optional but Recommended)
To make your main network actors pop out even more, color them based on how many connections they have:

   1. Go to the Appearance panel in the upper-left section of your screen.
   2. Select Nodes, then click the Ranking tab (represented by a tiny artist's palette icon).
   3. Choose In-Degree (number of incoming lines) or Out-Degree (number of outgoing lines) from the dropdown menu.
   4. Choose a color gradient (e.g., light blue to dark blue) and click Apply.

## Step 4: Finalizing for Export
To get a crisp, presentation-ready image of your network:

   1. Switch from the Overview tab to the Preview tab at the very top of your screen.
   2. On the left side, click Refresh to render the vector graphics version of your graph.
   3. In the preview settings panel, check the box next to Show Labels and re-click Refresh to ensure text displays.
   4. Click Export SVG/PDF/PNG at the bottom left of the preview panel to save your final network map.
