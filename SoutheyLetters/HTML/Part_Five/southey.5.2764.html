2764. Robert Southey to Mary Matilda Betham, 20 April 18161

My dear Miss Betham

I have no heart to examine & comment on these poems.2 The heaviest of all afflictions has fallen upon us – after a long illness – a dreadful season of hope & fear – we have lost our dear Herbert.3 Under such a visitation nothing but religion could support us.

Your portrait of him4 is now become invaluable. His mother has behaved with admirable fortitude. For myself I am shaken to the roots, & suffer severely in bodyxx. The mind is at it should be – & I find consolation there where only it can be found.

God bless you RS.

Keswick20 April 1816
