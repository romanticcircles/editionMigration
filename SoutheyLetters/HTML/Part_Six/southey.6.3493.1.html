3493.1 Robert Southey to [Charles Lloyd, Senior], 17 June [1820]*

15. Queen Anne Street. Cavendish Square. 17. June.Dear Sir

Will you allow me to request a favour, for which request the case itself will be the best excuse & apology.

My fathers sister, almost in her seventieth year, has been at length prevailed upon to undertake a journey from Xxxx Taunton to Keswick, to see mine & my brothers family. I am to meet her at Birmingham, with my friend Mr Nash, & we shall proceed from thence by chaises, at such a rate as may not fatigue her. I should feel it as an act of particular kindness if you would receive her for one night under your roof,1 – because she would not be likely to obtain much rest at an inn in your great & busy city. She will arrive in the stage from Bristol on Wednesday the 28th, & we intend to join her by the London mail on the day following.

I write this in great haste, or I would reply to some parts of the last letter with which you favoured me, & for which I can now only return my thanks. I am so involved in engagements of various kinds that I have scarcely time either for food or sleep. I saw Charles some days ago, & was delighted at seeing him in all respects so well. Sophia also was looking in better health, & happier than I had seen her for many years.

Believe me my dear Sirrespectfully & truly yoursRobert Southey.

Endorsement: R Southey 17/6 1820 –
MS: Pforzheimer Collection, New York Public Library, Misc 4428. ALS; 2p.
