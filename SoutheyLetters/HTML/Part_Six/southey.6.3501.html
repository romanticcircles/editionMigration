3501. Robert Southey to [James Heywood] Markland, 28 June 18201

Dear Sir

I return your Mysteries unopened, – not having had an hour which I could appropriate to their perusal. This would vex me, if I did not expect to have an opportunity very soon, of examining the whole series of the Club-publications.2

Thank you for the print. – Excuse this hasty note written with a trembling hand, after packing up for my departure from London, – & believe me

Dear Sir yrs respectfully Robert Southey.

28 June. 1820.

Notes

1.  Address: To/ — Markland Esqre/ Garden Walk/ Temple
Endorsement: 28 June 1820/ R. Southey Esq./ On returning Chester Mysteries.
MS: Cheshire Archives and Local Studies Service, Chester, D5154/78. ALS; 2p.
Unpublished. Back

2.  Markland had edited the volume of the Chester Mysteries. De Deluvio Noe. De Occisione Innocentium published by the Roxburghe Club in 1818. The Roxburghe Club was founded in 1812 as a society of bibliophiles with a membership limited to 40. It issued handsomely produced books on a regular basis. Back
