4129. Robert Southey to Grosvenor Charles Bedford, 8 February [1824]*

My dear G.

Any farther arrangement with Chantrey1 is impracticable on my part, even if it were otherwise on his. I am sorry for it, for I should like much to have past a few hours in his company. My place is taken for Friday, – will you give me a beef stake on Tuesday, & ask nobody to meet me, that I may have a quiet comfortable evening with you & the Magister Rotulorum & Miss Page? – Answer by a line to Q. Anne Street, whither I return that morning.

God bless you RS.

Richmond. Sunday 8 Feby

Notes

Address: To/ G. C. Bedford Esqre./ 9 Stafford Row <Exchequer>/ Buckingham Gate <Westminster>
Postmarks: [partial] HMOND/ Mg/ 9 FE/ 1824; 12. NOON. 12/ FE. 9/ 1824
Endorsements: 8 Feb 1824; 8 Feby 1824
MS: Bodleian Library, MS Eng. Lett. c. 26. ALS; 2p.
