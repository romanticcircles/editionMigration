2665b.Robert Southey to Grosvenor Charles Bedford, 29 October 1815 [English translation]*

Behold, my friend I am here On account of which I am glad; So, as I believe, you are reading Because it is a delightful thing After a sorrowful passage Over the stormy sea (Oh miserable me, it was all night One voice of vomiting!) Spit out by an angry sea On the English shore, And there I found with great joy Your letter with money. Now we are staying at Vardon’s house Our friend, a good traveller; And from there tomorrow, If I make good speed, Perhaps I will see you again. Give my love to your mother To Miss Page and your brother. This missive Classical and festive You may put with many others Farewell, O beloved head!

Greenwich. Sunday night. 29 Oct. 1815

Notes

Address: To/ G. C. Bedford Esqr/ Exchequer/ Westminster.
Postmark: 7 o’clock/ OC 30/ 1815 NT
Endorsement: Octor. 29. 1815. Greenwich/ Macaronic
