2182. Robert Southey to John Murray, 24 November 18121

Keswick.Nov. 24. 1812.My dear Sir

The Spirit is moving me strongly toward the Book of the Church,2 with which I shall make rapid progress as soon as Nelson3 is thro the press. There is a history of the <English> Catholics by Dodd4 which I wish you could procure for me, as I have reason to think it would afford me some materials. I do not know the exact title of the book, & x but it is a well-known book among the Catholics.

I hope my article upon the poor5 will be in time. It is so far advanced that I shall send off the greater part of it by the end of the week. I went to it with fear & trembling, – but shall not get thro it the worse for this sense of the importance of the subject.

There is copy of Nelson ready, which I can frank up if Mr Abbott should be re-elected Speaker.6 The papers throw some doubt upon this, – if he should not may I inclose it to Mr Croker? The manuscript will be as nearly as possible of the extent I calculated. – Would it not give the book an additional interest to have the words Horatio Nelson under the portrait given in a facsimile of his own writing?7

Believe me my dear Sir yrs very truly Robert Southey.

Send me when you have an opportunity young Mr Downmans poem,8 which his father is very desirous that I should see.
